Privacy Policy

Vast Research Backup is a personal research tool operated by its owner.

Google Drive Access

The application uses the Google Drive API with the drive.file scope to upload, retrieve, and manage research datasets, experiment results, and model checkpoints.

Data Storage and Processing

Google OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's Windows computer. Research files are stored in Google Drive and may be transferred to rented GPU servers for research computations.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell user data or share it for advertising purposes. Files are transferred only as needed for the owner's research workflow.

Data Retention and Deletion

Research files are retained until the owner deletes them. Google access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
